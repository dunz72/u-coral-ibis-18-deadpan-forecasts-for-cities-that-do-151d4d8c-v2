One absurd but believable weather line for a city that does not exist, read deadpan.

> In Bumbletown, expect a high of 23 degrees, scattered showers of confetti, and winds at 18 miles per hour, with visibility down to three confused pigeons.

## How it works

`main.py` is a standard-library-only copier: it reads `entry.txt` sitting next to it and copies it byte-identical to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, verifying the copy with SHA-256 before exiting. No third-party libraries are used.
